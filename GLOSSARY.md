# DJ Set Planning

The language used to plan a DJ performance through track selection, sequencing, and changes in energy.

## Language

**Set plan**:
An editable, ordered selection of tracks intended for a DJ to perform, with suggested transitions and an explanation of each track's role.
_Avoid_: Audio mix, playlist when referring to the complete performance plan

**Tracklist**:
The ordered list of tracks within a set plan.
_Avoid_: Mix

**Energy arc**:
The intended progression of intensity across a set, including rises, peaks, pullbacks, and the ending.
_Avoid_: BPM curve

**Phase**:
A section of the energy arc with a distinct purpose, such as establishing the groove, building intensity, reaching a peak, providing a breather, or landing the set.

**Must-include track**:
A track the DJ explicitly requires in a proposed set plan.
_Avoid_: Seed track when inclusion is required

**Performance context**:
The occasion or slot for which a set is being prepared.

**Music discovery**:
Finding candidate tracks beyond those the DJ explicitly supplies for a set.

**Genre emphasis**:
The desired prominence of a selected genre within a set plan, without requiring equal representation of every selected genre.

**Locked track**:
A track in an existing set plan that must remain included when the plan is regenerated.
_Avoid_: Must-include track when referring specifically to a lock applied during editing

**Position pin**:
A constraint that keeps a track in its chosen position in the tracklist.
_Avoid_: Lock when referring specifically to a fixed position

**Planned play time**:
The intended amount of a track played during the performance, which may be shorter than its full recording.

**Target energy**:
The desired intensity at a point in the energy arc, expressed on the user's 1–10 scale.
_Avoid_: Track energy when describing the desired arc rather than assessing a recording

**Track energy estimate**:
An editable assessment of a particular recording's perceived intensity, accompanied by its basis and confidence. It is distinct from the desired energy at the track's position in a set.
_Avoid_: Measured energy when the assessment is not derived from a defined measurement

**Starter catalog**:
The vetted collection of real tracks available for discovery and set planning in the first version.

**Transition overlap**:
The time during which adjacent tracks play simultaneously. It is counted once when calculating total set duration.

**Local library match**:
An association between a recommended recording and the same track version in the DJ's imported local library.
_Avoid_: Owned track when a matching local-library entry has not been established
